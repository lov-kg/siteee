Common questions

Taxis load from the Mamelodi hubs: Denneboom Station Main Taxi Rank as the primary operational hub, the Mams Mall Taxi Facility in Mahube Valley, and Tshwane Regional Mall Taxi Rank.

The association holds operating licences for approximately 35 registered local and regional routes, travelling along Tsamaya Road, Stormvoël Road and Hans Strydom (Solomon Mahlangu) Drive.

Local trips within Mamelodi are R17, scholars in uniform are R13, trips to Town (Pretoria CBD) are R28, and scholars to Town are R21. Fares are set with MATA, so confirm the current amount at the rank.

Regional and long-distance services run to Bronkhorstspruit, KwaMhlanga, Valbank, Monsterlus, Mabopane and Groblersdal. Local destinations include Belle Ombre Main Taxi Rank, Bloed Street, Silverton, East Lynne, Kilner Park, Queenswood, Mayville Mall, Akasia and Nellmapius.

Call 012 285 0195 or 012 801 3751, email malldtaxi@gmail.com, or message the Email line on . The head office is on Tsamaya Road & Solomon Mahlangu Drive, Mamelodi, Pretoria, 0122.

MATA is the Mamelodi Amalgamated Taxi Association, MALLDTA's sister organisation. The two work closely together to synchronise commuter prices, and MATA co-manages the Mams Mall Taxi Facility with the City of Tshwane.

MALLDTA operates under registration number RASGPA0333786. Shared operations can be reached or verified through the Tshwane Regional Mall information desk, or by telephone on 012 285 0195 or 012 801 3751.

Daily local and suburban commuter trips, regional and long-distance travel out of the district, loading from three Mamelodi hubs, and membership enquiries for operators.
