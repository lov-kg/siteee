About the association

Built around the people who move through Mamelodi

Mamelodi Local Long Distance Taxi Association is a registered minibus taxi association based in Mamelodi, City of Tshwane. We hold operating licences for approximately 35 routes and work alongside MATA to keep commuter fares consistent.

Registered RASGPA0333786 ~35 routes 3 Mamelodi hubs
Who we are

An association, not just a service

MALLDTA exists to coordinate taxi operations for the Mamelodi area — allocating routes, supporting the ranks where vehicles load, and representing the interests of the operators who run them.

Our registration number is RASGPA0333786. Day to day, that work happens through the office on Tsamaya Road and at the hubs our members load from.

Where we operate

Our network, hub by hub

Three loading points carry Mamelodi's taxi traffic, each with a different role.

01

Denneboom Station Main Taxi Rank

The association's primary operational hub. Most local loads and the bulk of the corridor traffic to town move through here.

02

Mams Mall Taxi Facility

Situated in Mahube Valley and co-managed with the City of Tshwane and MATA, connecting Mamelodi to mall-bound and regional traffic.

03

Tshwane Regional Mall Taxi Rank

A regional hub serving longer runs. Shared operations can be reached or verified here with the information desk.

MATA

Working with our sister organisation

MATA — the Mamelodi Amalgamated Taxi Association — is our sister organisation. The two work closely together to synchronise commuter prices, and MATA co-manages the Mams Mall Taxi Facility with the City of Tshwane.

Shared operations can also be reached or verified through the Tshwane Regional Mall information desk, or directly with our office.
