How it works

Three steps to your destination

Tell us where

Send an enquiry with your route or destination, or simply head to your nearest hub.

Load at the hub

Taxis load at Denneboom Station, the Mams Mall Taxi Facility or Tshwane Regional Mall.

Pay the fare

Cash fares, agreed with MATA. Scholars in uniform travel at the reduced rate.

Ready when you are

Need a taxi from Mamelodi?

Tell us the route, the hub or the destination and we will point you to the right taxi. For anything urgent, telephone is fastest.
